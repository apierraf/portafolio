01 / About

From a Cuban university classroom to shipping national apps.

Adrian's path into mobile started as a professor of Programming and Databases at Universidad Minero Metalúrgica de Moa, where he also tutored a thesis on a mobile app for herbarium data collection — the first sign of where his career was headed.

He then spent close to a decade as a technical lead and project manager on national-scale mobile projects in Cuba — at CITMATEL and later leading the ENTUMOVIL mobile development area at DESOFT — shipping apps used by government institutions, national media, and millions of everyday users.

More recently he's worked with teams in the US and Canada on Flutter products, and stays active as a full-stack web developer across React, Vue, Angular and Node.
